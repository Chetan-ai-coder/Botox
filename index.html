The Treatment

What Is
Botulinum Toxin?

Botulinum toxin type A is a well-established, non-surgical aesthetic treatment widely used in anti-aging medicine to reduce dynamic facial wrinkles and help prevent deeper lines over time.

By temporarily relaxing targeted facial muscles, it softens expression lines while preserving natural movement and facial harmony.

Treatment is tailored to your facial anatomy and aesthetic goals, ensuring natural-looking results.

Treatment Zones

Facial Aesthetic Treatments

Forehead Lines
Relaxes the frontalis muscle to smooth horizontal forehead lines. Treatment preserves natural expression while eliminating deep creases from repeated raising of the brows.
Frown Lines (Glabellar Lines)
Targets the "11 lines" between the eyebrows caused by frowning. One of the most requested areas — results in a visibly more relaxed and approachable appearance.
Brow Lift (Non-Surgical)
Strategic injections subtly elevate the brow arch, creating a lifting effect without surgery. Brightens the eye area and restores a youthful arch.
Crow's Feet
Softens the fine lines radiating from the outer corners of the eyes. Carefully dosed to preserve natural smiling expression.
Lip Flip
Relaxes the orbicularis oris muscle to create a subtle upper lip eversion — giving the appearance of a fuller lip without filler.
Gummy Smile
Reduces excessive gum show when smiling by relaxing the levator labii superioris alaeque nasi muscle.
Bunny Lines · Nefertiti Lift · Marionette Lines
Bunny lines on the nose bridge, the Nefertiti neck lift, and downturned mouth corners — all addressable with precise toxin placement.

Medical & Functional Treatments

Masseter Reduction (Facial Slimming)
Slims a square jaw by reducing the bulk of the masseter muscle. Doubles as treatment for bruxism (teeth grinding).
Excessive Sweating (Hyperhidrosis)
Blocks the nerve signals that activate sweat glands — highly effective for underarms, hands, and feet. Results last 6–9 months.
Migraine Treatment · Trapezius Botox
Therapeutic botox for chronic migraine sufferers and neck tension. Trapezius reduction ("Barbie Tox") slims the shoulder-neck profile.
